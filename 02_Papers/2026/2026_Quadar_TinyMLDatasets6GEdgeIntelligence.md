# TinyML Datasets as Enablers of 6G Edge Intelligence: Key Insights and Research Gaps

**Full citation:** Quadar, N., Chehri, A., Debaque, B. (2026). TinyML Datasets as Enablers of 6G Edge Intelligence: Key Insights and Research Gaps. IEEE Wireless Communications, 33(2).

**PDF:** [IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11370394/)

**Linked concepts:** [[TinyML_Reference_Datasets]]

## Abstract summary

A dedicated survey of the datasets underpinning TinyML development, arguing that limited availability of representative datasets is a significant constraint on TinyML's role as a cornerstone of distributed 6G edge intelligence, and identifying research gaps in current dataset coverage.

## Research problem

TinyML dataset landscape had not been systematically surveyed as its own topic, despite dataset limitations being a genuine bottleneck for developing and validating TinyML models for emerging 6G-era edge intelligence use cases.

## Key idea

Treat TinyML datasets as a first-class research object in their own right — surveying coverage, gaps, and quality — rather than as an incidental detail of individual application papers.

## Technical contribution

A structured review of TinyML dataset landscape with explicit gap analysis, framed around 6G edge-intelligence requirements (ubiquitous, low-latency, distributed inference at scale).

## Experimental methodology

Literature survey and dataset landscape analysis.

## Results

Identifies specific under-covered dataset categories and argues dataset scarcity/quality is a limiting factor for TinyML's growth into 6G-scale distributed edge intelligence deployments.

## Comparison with the state of the art

The first dedicated survey-level anchor for this concept as a topic in its own right, directly relevant to the concept's own open question about whether Speech Commands/Visual Wake Words remain representative as the field's workload mix shifts.

## Strengths

Recent (2026), directly scoped to this concept's exact topic (TinyML datasets), forward-looking toward emerging 6G/distributed deployment contexts.

## Weaknesses

Survey only, no new dataset or empirical validation; 6G framing is speculative/forward-looking rather than grounded in currently deployed systems.

## Limitations

Broad survey scope may not go deep on any single dataset's limitations.
